# 1. Executive Summary

The FNP Sales Analysis project focuses on understanding business sales performance, revenue generation, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and geographical order distribution through data analysis and visualization.

The primary objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help understand sales trends, identify high-performing products and occasions, analyze customer spending patterns, and recognize opportunities for business improvement.

The project followed a structured data analytics workflow, starting with data identification and data profiling, followed by data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, and data visualization.

Initially, I identified the relevant datasets and examined the available columns, data types, and business attributes required for analysis. I then performed data profiling to understand the dataset structure, evaluate data quality, and identify areas requiring attention.

After profiling, I worked on data cleaning and transformation to prepare the data for accurate analysis. The prepared data was then organized through data modeling to support meaningful calculations and reporting.

Using Microsoft Excel, I analyzed different dimensions of sales performance, including revenue by occasion, product category, month, and order hour. I also identified the top five products based on revenue and the top ten cities based on order volume.

The analysis covered 1,000 total orders and generated a total revenue of ₹35,20,984. The dashboard also highlights an order-to-delivery time KPI of 5.53 and average customer spending of ₹3,520.98.

The findings revealed that Anniversary was the highest revenue-generating individual occasion, Colors was the leading product category, and August recorded the highest monthly revenue.

Finally, I developed a sales dashboard with interactive date and occasion filters to make the analysis easier to explore and understand.

This project demonstrates the complete data analytics process, from understanding raw business data to preparing, analyzing, and presenting meaningful insights for business decision-making.

| KPI | Value |

| Total Orders | 1,000 |

**Total Orders:** Represents the total number of orders recorded in the dataset.

**Total Revenue:** Represents the overall revenue generated through sales.

**Order-Delivery Time:** Represents the order-to-delivery time metric displayed in the dashboard. The measurement unit should be confirmed before reporting it as days or another unit.

**Average Customer Spending:** Represents the average customer spending displayed in the dashboard.

# 6. Detailed Sales Analysis and Business Insights

## 6.1 Revenue by Occasions

Analyzed revenue generated across different occasions to identify which celebrations contribute significantly to overall sales.

Occasions analyzed:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day

### Key Findings

- Anniversary generates the highest revenue among the individual occasions, at approximately ₹6.7 lakh.
- Raksha Bandhan contributes approximately ₹6.3 lakh.
- Holi generates approximately ₹5.7 lakh.
- Birthday and Valentine's Day contribute comparatively lower revenue.

### Business Insight

Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan, and Holi are important revenue-generating occasions. The business can prioritize seasonal marketing campaigns, promotional offers, and inventory planning around these occasions.

## 6.2 Revenue by Product Category

Analyzed revenue generated by different product categories to understand product-level performance.

Categories analyzed:

- Colors
- Sweets
- Cake
- Soft Toys
- Plants
- Raksha Bandhan
- Mugs

### Key Findings

- Colors is the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately ₹10 lakh.
- Sweets contributes approximately ₹7.3 lakh.
- Soft Toys contributes approximately ₹7.3 lakh.
- Cake contributes approximately ₹3.2 lakh.
- Plants, Raksha Bandhan products, and Mugs contribute comparatively lower revenue.

### Business Insight

Colors, Sweets, and Soft Toys are major revenue contributors. Maintaining product availability and developing promotional strategies around these categories could help improve sales performance.

## 6.3 Revenue by Hour (Order Time)

Analyzed hourly revenue patterns to understand how customer purchasing activity changes throughout the day.

### Key Findings

- Revenue fluctuates across different hours.
- Around 8 AM records the highest revenue, at approximately ₹2.25 lakh.
- Another noticeable peak occurs around 1 AM.
- Revenue varies throughout the remaining hours.

### Business Insight

Understanding hourly purchasing patterns can help identify suitable periods for promotional campaigns, customer engagement activities, and operational planning.

## 6.4 Revenue by Months

Analyzed monthly revenue distribution to identify seasonal trends and changes in business performance.

### Key Findings

- August records the highest monthly revenue, at approximately ₹7.3 lakh.
- February follows with approximately ₹7 lakh.
- March contributes approximately ₹5.1 lakh.
- November generates approximately ₹4.4 lakh.
- Other months record comparatively lower revenue.

### Business Insight

Monthly revenue analysis helps identify high-performing periods and seasonal demand patterns. These findings can support better marketing budget allocation, sales planning, and inventory management.

## 6.5 Top 5 Products by Revenue

Identified the five highest revenue-generating products to understand product-level sales performance.

### Top 5 Products

1. Magnam Set
2. Quia Gift
3. Dolores Gift
4. Harum Pack
5. Deserunt Box

### Key Finding

Magnam Set leads the top five products with approximately ₹1.2 lakh in revenue.

### Business Insight

Identifying high-revenue products helps management understand product demand and prioritize inventory planning, product promotions, and sales strategies.

## 6.6 Top 10 Cities by Orders

Analyzed geographical order distribution to identify cities with higher customer purchasing activity.

### Key Findings

- Imphal records the highest order volume, with approximately 29 orders.
- Dhanbad follows with approximately 28 orders.
- Kannur contributes approximately 27 orders.
- Haridwar records approximately 24 orders.
- Other cities demonstrate varying levels of order volume.

### Business Insight

City-wise order analysis helps identify stronger geographical markets and potential opportunities for regional marketing and customer acquisition.
